Optimization Logic

Winner selection uses market/model probability first. CBS pick percentage is treated as field ownership. Confidence 16→1 is assigned across the whole card, not game-by-game. When no market spread is available, the card is marked FIELD ONLY rather than pretending CBS ownership is a predictive model.
