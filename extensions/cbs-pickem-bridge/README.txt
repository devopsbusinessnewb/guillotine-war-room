CBS Pick'em IQ Bridge v0.3

WHAT CHANGED
- One-click Scan CBS Picks + Standings.
- Automatically visits the pool Picks page and Weekly Standings page.
- Keeps only CBS GraphQL responses instead of ad/network noise.
- Adds operationName and variables to each captured GraphQL response.
- Exports a compact diagnostic summary plus the captured CBS payloads.

INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Unzip this package.
2. Open chrome://extensions.
3. Remove v0.2.
4. Click Load unpacked and choose the cbs_pickem_iq_bridge_v0_3 folder.
5. Open your CBS Pick'em pool and refresh once.
6. Click the extension and choose Scan CBS Picks + Standings.
7. Wait about 10 seconds. Open the extension again if the popup closes.
8. When it says SCAN COMPLETE, click Export clean CBS data.
9. Upload the JSON to ChatGPT.
